Warehouse Dock Booking Tool

Carriers book dock door appointments, the warehouse team runs the day from a live operations view, and managers use the dashboard to track dock utilization, carrier reliability, and turn times.

Book an appointment

Pick a weekday and time slot. Live availability shows how many of the 4 dock doors are still open, and a door is assigned automatically.

Run the dock

Filter appointments, check trucks in and out, record no-shows and cancellations, and export to CSV.

Analyze performance

KPIs against the prior period, a utilization heatmap, a carrier scorecard, and findings written from the data.

Business questions this answers

  • Are we using our dock capacity well, and where are the peaks and gaps?
  • Which carriers arrive late or don't show up, and how much does it cost us?
  • What drives slow turn times at the door?
  • Can booking policy (lead time, reminders) reduce wasted slots?
